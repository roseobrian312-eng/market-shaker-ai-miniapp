About this app

Market Shaker AI • v5 research + tick pipeline

How to use it

  1. Select an OTC asset.
  2. Tap Scan Market to request recent candles.
  3. Review the analysis, freshness and signal, if any.
  4. Independently decide whether to place a trade in Pocket Option.

Market data

OTCharts is an independent read-only provider, not an official Pocket Option trading API. There is no access to your account, balance, orders or login.

Data requests use a protected server-side key. If the key is not configured, the app displays a clear error instead of showing simulated signals.

Strategy research

Test baseline EMA/RSI against trend + pullback + confirmation. Short-expiry research uses closed candles and a simulated next-candle entry, not actual broker order fills.

No validated machine-learning live trading model or automatic trading is connected. The Strategy Lab reports modeled results and does not claim a guaranteed or verified live win rate.
